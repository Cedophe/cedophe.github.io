# Surface brief
Target: `index.html`
Mode: experience

## Audience and job
Recruiters, hiring managers, and infrastructure decision-makers in francophone and anglophone West Africa. They need to see a field-tested network and cybersecurity engineer, understand the practitioner-educator pairing, verify concrete deployments and credentials, and make contact. French remains the inline default; English is a full mirror.

## Action
Primary: open a channel (contact section, mailto/tel, copy controls). Secondary: download the CV once `assets/cv.pdf` exists. The contact form stays a client-side demonstration, never a delivery mechanism.

## Proof and constraints
Use only the confirmed facts: IMPROTECH since 2023, Palo Alto NGFW for 500+ users, FortiGate/FortiManager across 16+ branches, the Python inventory tool, 50+ engineers trained, CCNA, CCNP Enterprise 350-401, AZ-104, NSE 3, the 2021 Benin internship, and the confirmed contact/profile links. Never invent screenshots, badges, testimonials, live links, or a CV. Static vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no build step or new dependencies beyond the two Google Fonts families. All user-visible strings need FR/EN parity; theme and language persistence stay flash-free. Motion must stop under reduced motion with all content visible.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A network engineer's proof presented as a late-modern civic prospectus, not a case file, cyber cliché, or card-grid template. The page is one continuous bone-white document with a single signal ribbon; the category's centered hero, stat row, and dashboard tiles are refused.

OWN-WORLD: Bone-white ground (#F7F7FA/#FFFFFF), ink navy (#1B1547), one violet action (#6B5CE7), and a teal-to-lilac-to-violet silk ribbon. Hairline rules (#E6E7EE), 1180px ruled bands, 12px panels, 8px controls, Albert Sans at 400/500 for display and reading, JetBrains Mono for metadata. No paper, desk, grain, or heavy shadows. Dark mode is a deep-indigo night prospectus, not a tint.

STORY: The visitor sees 500+ users, 16+ branches, and 50+ trained engineers as a coherent infrastructure record, understands the practitioner-educator pairing, then opens a channel or checks the credentials.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 72px sticky top bar carries the logo, section links, FR/EN, theme, and one violet contact action. Below, a 48/52 hero: two-line display type on the left with the second line violet; deck and action pair beneath. The right is an inline SVG silk ribbon crossing a ghosted topology, with one dark network-core object. A five-cell facts band with a stack disclosure sits beneath the hero, followed by the representative multi-site signal path. Contact is the primary action; CV is the secondary.

FORM: Pinned catalog world `civic-bureau-prospectus-late-modern`; seed: none (pinned world, no concept roll).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
